Can a character learn to move through physically simulated muscles rather than traditional animation?

PROJECT DEMO ▶
02 / THE QUESTION

What happens when animation becomes a physical problem?

Traditional character animation describes how a character should move. For this project, I wanted to explore the opposite approach: give the character a physical body, control it through simulated muscles, and let the movement emerge from the interaction between the controller and the physics simulation.

The long-term goal is to investigate whether physically plausible procedural animation can be learned rather than explicitly authored.

The resulting movement feeds back into the learning process, creating an iterative loop between control, physical simulation and evaluation.

04 / PHYSICS SYSTEM

Building the body before teaching it to move.

Before introducing learning, the character needs a stable physical representation. Each body part has mass and physical constraints, while muscles apply forces that influence the resulting motion.

PHYSICS DEMO / SCREENSHOT
MASS

Mass distribution influences how forces propagate through the character and how difficult different movements are to perform.

CONSTRAINTS

Joint constraints limit the physically possible motion and provide the foundation for stable character behaviour.

05 / AUTHORING TOOL

Making the simulation authorable.

A physically simulated character quickly becomes difficult to manage directly through raw data. I therefore built an editor tool to create and manage muscles and mass distributions directly inside Unreal Engine.

MUSCLE AUTHORING TOOL
01

Muscle creation

Define muscles and associate them with the character's physical structure.

02

Mass management

Configure and visualize the mass distribution used by the simulation.

03

Visualization

Make otherwise invisible physical data visible while authoring and debugging.
